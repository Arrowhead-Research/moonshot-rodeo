# Moonshot Rodeo research and draft decisions

Researched October 29, 2026 using the Arrowhead Research Google Drive account (josh@arrowheadresearch.org). Searched `moonshot` and `rodeo`, reviewed the event folder and retrospective assets, and read the social media guide, retrospective, post-event LinkedIn copy, organizational one-pager, and project coordination guide. The outreach document was empty. The digital program is image-based; text extraction returned no readable text.

## Grounded findings

- Moonshot Rodeo is Arrowhead Research's prototype showcase, distinct from Black Relay's technical build/field-testing program.
- Audience and talent focus: nontraditional military and intelligence community technical talent; connections across defense, academia, venture, policy, and industry.
- February 6, 2026 event: Virginia Tech Innovation Campus, Alexandria, Virginia. Promotional hours were 9:00 AM–1:30 PM.
- Hosted with Virginia Tech and the Boeing Center for Veteran Transition and Military Families.
- Retrospective confirms seven prototype presentations, eight-minute format, structured cross-sector expert feedback.
- Dr. Alex Miller delivered the keynote and served on the feedback panel. His title is described in historical context, not as a verified current appointment.
- Prototype areas: autonomous unmanned systems; AI-augmented open-source development; embedded systems security; Android malware detection; LoRa personnel tracking; contested RF field networking; spectrum exploitation research.
- Themes: operational problems into engineering architectures; low-cost, high-impact prototyping; academic rigor; technical talent development; explaining tradeoffs and operational value.
- Contact: info@arrowheadresearch.org.
- No future date or open application round was established by the retrieved materials. Old Donorbox ticket sales are not used as a current CTA.

## Editorial decisions

Evergreen program introduction with a clearly historical 2026 recap. Original draft headline: “From the field. To the forefront.” Uses the source tagline “Engineering innovation from the battlefield.” Black Relay visual cues: dark green-black, mint accents, oversized headings, mission-first structure, anchored navigation, photography, FAQ. Contact links are real mailto actions. No invented future dates, registration status, attendance totals, quotes, or sponsorship commitments. Internal contact lists, budgets, and outreach notes are not included in the served site.

## Visual revision

Following feedback, replaced the Black Relay-derived dark aesthetic with warm ivory, burnt orange, serif display typography, a printed-photo composition, and a typographic manifesto. The two event photographs now appear exactly once each. CSS/JS remain embedded in the served HTML; `build.py` synchronizes the editable source template and styles.
