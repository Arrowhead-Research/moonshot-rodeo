Moonshot Rodeo brings nontraditional military, intelligence-community, and veteran technical talent into the same room as experts from defense, academia, industry, venture, and policy.

EVENT DETAILS

Common questions.

info@arrowheadresearch.org
What is Moonshot Rodeo?+

An Arrowhead Research event for nontraditional military, intelligence-community, and veteran technical talent. Participants can join the optional Build Sprint, bring an existing project, or present a Field Solution. They present their work and defend their approach to a panel of experts who give structured feedback.

Do I need to join the build sprint?+

No. The two-week build sprint is optional. Apply to build with equipment and expert support, bring an existing project, or present a creative solution to a problem in the field. All three ways to present share the same stage and expert panel. Read the sprint details.

Do I need a physical prototype?+

No. We also welcome presentations on creative solutions to real problems, like field workarounds, new processes, or improvised tools. If you solved something hard, it fits.

What kinds of projects fit?+

The 2026 projects covered autonomy, software, security, tracking, and RF systems. We also welcome field workarounds, processes, tactics, and improvised tools. The common thread is solving real operational problems.

When is the next event?+

The 2026 event took place February 6. Contact Arrowhead for future dates and submission details.

Can I help without a prototype?+

Yes. You can present a field solution without a physical prototype, or reach out about mentorship or partnership opportunities.
