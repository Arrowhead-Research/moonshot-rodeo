Track 02 · Bring Your Own

Already have a prototype? Bring it.

This track is for participants who already have a prototype and want to present that project at Moonshot Rodeo without joining the Build Sprint. You do not need to build it during the sprint.

At the event, you present your work to a panel of experts in engineering, research, and industry, who question the approach and give structured feedback.

Use the application form to share a short description of your project. Moonshot Rodeo is February 4, 2027.

Track 03 · Field Solutions

Solved it in the field? That counts.

This track is for presentations that may not be a physical prototype. We welcome creative solutions to real problems in the field, such as workarounds, processes, tactics, or improvised tools. If you solved something hard, it fits.

Prepare to walk the panel through your thinking: how you solved the problem and why you approached it that way. You do not need a physical device.

At the event, you present your field solution to the same expert panel as the other tracks.
