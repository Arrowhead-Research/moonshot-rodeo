Bring Your Own

This track is for participants who already have a prototype and want to present that project at Moonshot Rodeo without joining the Build Sprint.

Bring the project you already have. You do not need to build it during the sprint.

At the event, you present your work and defend your approach to a panel of experts in engineering, research, and industry, who question the approach and give structured feedback. All three ways to present share the same stage and expert panel.

To apply, email Arrowhead Research with a short description of your project. Moonshot Rodeo is February 4, 2027. Submission details are to be confirmed.

Apply by email

Field Solutions

This track is for presentations that may not be a physical prototype. We welcome creative solutions to real problems in the field, such as workarounds, processes, tactics, or improvised tools. If you solved something hard, it fits.

Prepare to walk the panel through your thinking: how you solved the problem and why you approached it that way. You do not need a physical device.

At the event, you present your field solution and defend your approach to the same expert panel as the other tracks. All three ways to present share the same stage and expert panel.

To apply, email Arrowhead Research. Moonshot Rodeo is February 4, 2027. Submission details are to be confirmed.
