HOW IT WORKS

Apply, build,
and present.

Apply with your project.

Tell Arrowhead what you want to build and what equipment or advice would help.

Join the two-week sprint.

Use the build period before the event to develop your prototype with focused time and support.

Present and defend your prototype.

Participants defend their prototype to a panel of experts in engineering, research, and industry, who question the design and give structured feedback.

All three ways to present share the same stage: join the Build Sprint, bring an existing project, or present a Field Solution.
